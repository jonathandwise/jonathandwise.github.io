02 / Featured PhD Research

Nuclear Roles of Argonaute-1 in Transcript Processing & Mitosis

Doctoral dissertation completed at the Irell & Manella Graduate School of Biological Sciences, City of Hope (Lab of Dr. Arthur D. Riggs, 2019). Select a module below to explore the hypotheses, methods, and key quantitative findings.

Chapter 4 · Isoform Regulation & Cell Division

AGO1 Is Required for Full-Length CENPN Expression & Chromosome Alignment

Transcriptome profiling of S-phase synchronized HeLa cells revealed that depleting AGO1—but not AGO2—selectively ablates expression of the full-length isoform of Centromere Protein N (CENPN-long, 40 kDa), leaving only a truncated isoform (CENPN-short, 24 kDa) that lacks the C-terminal domain required to assemble the 16-subunit CCAN kinetochore complex.

  • Optimized combined reverse siRNA transfection and double thymidine block synchronization, achieving ~89% AGO1 mRNA and ~71% protein knockdown without appreciable added cell death.
  • Designed isoform-specific qRT-PCR assays and confirmed specificity using a second independent siRNA duplex.
  • Tested the resulting functional hypothesis via immunofluorescence microscopy (DAPI/Hoechst DNA + AlexaFluor488 α-tubulin mitotic spindle): ~68% of AGO1-depleted dividing cells (n = 123) exhibited metaphase chromosome misalignment vs. ~8% of siCTRL cells (n = 89).
  • Proposed a mechanistic link to chromosomal instability in hypoxic tumor microenvironments where hypoxia-induced miRNAs downregulate AGO1.

Chapter 3 · Dual-Library RNA-seq

Selective Loss of Polyadenylated Histone Transcripts Upon AGO1 Depletion

Canonical replication-dependent histone transcripts are uniquely processed without poly(A) tails during S-phase. To investigate whether nuclear Argonaute proteins modulate histone 3′ UTR processing, I designed a dual-fraction RNA-seq workflow separating oligo(dT)-selected Poly(A)+ RNA from rRNA-depleted Poly(A)− RNA in mid-S-phase HeLa cells.

  • Confirmed strong replicate concordance via hierarchical clustering across all treatment and control groups.
  • Discovered that AGO1 knockdown reduced total Poly(A)+ histone read counts by 72% relative to siCTRL (vs. only 8% for AGO2 knockdown), while canonical non-polyadenylated histone transcripts (~99% of total) remained largely unchanged.
  • Performed a transcriptome-wide 50-bp sliding-window Differentially Expressed Region (DER) analysis (≥3-fold change), uncovering 1,049 AGO1-dependent DERs vs. 258 for AGO2—predominantly in unannotated intragenic and intergenic non-coding regions.
  • Formulated a transcriptional elongation-rate model connecting AGO1 chromatin occupancy to nascent histone pre-mRNA folding and 3′-end choice.

Chapter 2 · Chromatin & Genomics

Genome-Wide Argonaute Promoter Occupancy & DHX36 Co-Localization

To test whether nuclear Argonaute proteins broadly regulate transcription at mammalian promoters, I mapped genome-wide AGO binding by ChIP-chip in differentiating mouse embryonic stem cells (mESCs, retinoic acid time course) and MCF7 human breast cancer cells.

  • Identified thousands of AGO-occupied promoters across mouse (NimbleGen 3×720K) and human (NimbleGen 2.1M Deluxe Promoter Array) genomes, with DAVID Gene Ontology revealing strong Day 6 enrichment in chromatin organization and histone gene clusters.
  • Integrated ChIP-chip peak scores with microarray expression fold-changes (R2 = 0.0014), demonstrating that promoter occupancy alone did not drive uniform transcriptional silencing—pivoting the project toward co-transcriptional processing.
  • Partnered with a postdoctoral colleague on a joint study revealing pervasive co-localization of AGO, the G-quadruplex helicase DHX36 (G4R1), and RNA Polymerase II at thousands of sites in MCF7 cells, including the MYC / CASC11 locus and Histone Clusters 1 & 2 across S-phase.

Appendix · Reagent Quality Control

Systematic Specificity Benchmarking of 9 Commercial AGO Antibodies

Because human Argonaute family members (AGO1–4) share ~92% sequence similarity by NCBI BLAST, unverified antibodies pose a major reproducibility risk. Before running isoform-specific assays, I independently tested 9 commercial antibodies against purified recombinant human AGO1–4 proteins and MCF7 whole-cell extracts using quantitative LI-COR Odyssey Western blots.

  • Identified widely marketed "isoform-specific" antibodies (including Millipore 07-599 and Abcam ab57113) that strongly cross-reacted across all four Argonaute proteins despite vendor claims.
  • Flagged molecular weight discrepancies in vendor datasheets for recombinant tagged AGO standards and verified true isoform specificity for downstream Western blot and ChIP workflows.
  • Established a validated antibody panel (e.g., Cell Signaling #5053 for AGO1, Wako 11-22033 & Abcam ab156870 for AGO2) adopted by the laboratory.
